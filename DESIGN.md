
## 12w. First-flight tutorial and gradual station tabs
Playtesters found the opening confusing, so a new career now starts with an 8-step guided run (`tutorial.js`):
1. fly to the planet ore deposit nearest the home station (ship spawns facing it), 2. hold DRILL for 12 units,
3. fly back and DOCK, 4. SELL ALL ORE, 5. open the newly revealed UPGRADES tab and install a recommended Mk1
(Mining Laser → Ion Engine → Cargo Pods; a Guild grant tops up credits if ever short), 6. UNDOCK, open the MAP,
pick the training wreck ("Drifting Hauler", placed ~1.5 km from the station) and choose AUTOPILOT, 7. hold SALVAGE
(fixed loot: ◈250 + 6 Machine Parts), 8. dock and sell. Then Wick explains the loop and the game opens up.
- An objective bar ("TRAINING n/8") and a pulsing ▶ waypoint marker show the current goal; key buttons pulse.
  Instructions are worded for the device in use (controller / touch / keyboard).
- While training: story comms are held and delivered after graduation; no radio chatter, outlaw raids, sector
  events, Accord scans or tariffs, story-arc triggers or debt clock (the Debtor's weekly marker starts at graduation).
  FIRE / MSL / TARGET buttons are hidden. The market lists only what is in the hold.
- The intro shows two short lines per origin plus a TUTORIAL: ON/OFF toggle; the pause menu has SKIP TUTORIAL.
- Station tabs open progressively for every new career (Market → Upgrades → Services at graduation → Contracts
  via its unlock → Shipyard at rank 1 / ◈12k / a second ship → Cantina from day 3 or first contract), each with a
  NEW badge until visited; Wick introduces Shipyard and Cantina. Feature-unlock messages are spaced ≥40 s apart.
- The Journal opens with WHAT NEXT? (next upgrade, take a contract, follow your story) until all three are done.
- Older saves skip the tutorial and keep every tab.
